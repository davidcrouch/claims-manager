What is an Assessment Job
A Builder Assessment job is allocated to you when an Insurer would like you to conduct an assessment of the damage. It is used to investigate and detail the cause of the damage and the repairs required. 

 

The information gathered is provided to the Insurer to assist them in making an informed decision on the outcome of a customer’s claim. This generally includes attending the site and completing a report and/or quote on the damages, to provide back to the Insurer.

The Builder Assessment job displays specific fields and data throughout the Job UI.

 

Banner and Header Fields
The Job banner and header fields sit at the top of every Builder Assessment job, and contain core information related to the claim.

 


 

 

Field

1

Who the job is allocated to (your company name)

The claim number

The risk address

2

Job reference

Combination of:

The claim number

The job suffix

3

Account

Displays the Insurance Company

4

Zone

Displays the Insurer’s service region associated with the claim

5

Status

Displays the current state of the job

*The job status is updated through automations

Please note that any field indicated as “updated by an automation” should not be manually updated by you.

Overview Tab
Whilst all jobs are made up of the same 6 tabs, the Overview tab contains and captures different data, depending on the job type.

 

For the Builder Assessment Job, the Overview tab captures data related to customer contact, site attendances and assessment submissions.

Overview & Contact Data
The Overview & Contact Data section contains fields to capture information related to the assessment job.

 


Builder Assessment - Overview & Contact Data section

 

Field

Used for

1

Vendor Job Number

Make Safe Required

Request Date

Displays the date the Insurer allocated the job to your company

Contact Date

Booked Date

Displays the date you finalised your appointment booking with the customer

Attendance Date

Submission Due Date

*This field is populated by an automation

Displays the date you submitted your report/quote to the Insurer for the first time

*This field is populated by an automation

13

Last Submission Date

Displays the last time you submitted your report/quote to the Insurer

*This field is populated by an automation
