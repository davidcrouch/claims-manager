What is a Make Safe Job?
A Builder Make Safe job is allocated to you when an Insurer requires you to temporarily make the property safe. It is used to complete temporary measures at the property to ensure the risk of harm to people/pets is prevented and/or further damage to the property is minimised. You will also have the ability to create your own Builder Make Safe job if you identify one is required.

Banner and Header Fields
The Job banner and header fields sit at the top of every Builder Make Safe job, and contain core information related to the claim.

The claim number

Job reference

Combination of:

The claim number

The job suffix

3

Account

Displays the Insurance Company

4

Zone

Displays the Insurer’s service region associated with the claim

5

Status

Displays the current state of the job

*The job status is updated through automations

Please note that any field indicated as “updated by an automation” should not be manually updated by you.

Overview Tab
Whilst all jobs consist of the same 6 tabs, the Overview tab contains and captures different data depending on the job type.

 

For the Builder Make Safe Job, the Overview tab captures data on customer contact, site attendance, and make safe completion.
