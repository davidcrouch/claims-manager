What is a Works Job?
A Builder Works job is considered the repair phase of a claim. Once a property has been assessed and the Insurer has approved your Builder Assessment quote, you can commence scheduling trades to repair the damage.

💡 Not sure what a Crunchwork Job is? Read Crunchwork Overview for a more detailed breakdown.

Builder Works Specifics

Builder Works Job

The Builder Works job displays specific fields and data throughout the Job UI.

 

Banner and Header Fields
The Job banner and header fields sit at the top of every Builder Assessment job, and contain core information related to the claim.


 

Field

Used for

1

Banner

Combination of:

Who the job is allocated to (your company name)

The claim number

The risk address

2

Job reference

Combination of:

The claim number

The job suffix

3

Account

Displays the Insurance Company

4

Zone

Displays the Insurer’s service region associated with the claim

5

Status

Displays the current state of the job

*The job status is updated through automations

Please note that any field indicated as “updated by an automation” should not be manually updated by you.

💡 Not sure what triggers each automation? Read Builder Works Flow.

Overview Tab
Whilst all jobs are made up of the same 6 tabs, the Overview tab contains and captures different data, depending on the job type.

 

For the Builder Works Job, the Overview tab captures data on scope signage, excess collection, repair dates and completion certificate uploads.

Builder Works - Overview & Contact Data section

 


​Field

Used for

1

Vendor Job Number

Request Date

Displays the date the Insurer allocated the job to your company

3

Excess Sent Date

Displays the date you sent the excess invoice to the customer (if excess was required).

Displays the date you provide the scope/contract to the customer to sign

*This field is populated by an automation

5

Scope Signed Date

Displays the date you received the signed scope/contract back from the customer

*This field is populated by an automation

6

Works Scheduled Date

Displays the date you initially finished scheduling repairs

*This field is populated by an automation

7

Estimated Start Date

Displays the date you have scheduled repairs to commence

8

Estimated Completion Date

Displays the date you have scheduled repairs to be finished

9

Works Commencement Date

Displays the date repairs commenced at the property

*This field is populated by an automation

10

Works Completion Date

Displays the date repairs were completed at the property

*This field is populated by an automation

11

Completion Certificate Upload Date

Displays the date you uploaded the signed completion certificate to the job

*This field is populated by an automation

12

Date Customer Confirmed Completion

Displays the date the customer verbally indicated the repairs were complete

13

Collect Excess

Displays if the Insurer has indicated that you are required to collect excess from the customer

14

Excess Collected Date

Displays the date you collected excess from the customer (if applicable)

*This field is populated by an automation

15

Excess Payment Collected

Displays if you have indicated excess has been collected

*This field is populated by an automation

16

Excess

Displays the excess amount

17

Auto Approval Applies

Displays if the Insurer has indicated ifyour delegated authority limit will be used for any Works variations

18

Claim Recommendation

The field is default-populated to the claim recommendation from your Assessment job

*This is required to be populated to facilitate auto-approval

19

Specialist Required

Field for you to populate to indicate if a specialist report is required

Please note that any field indicated as “populated by an automation” should not be manually populated by you.
