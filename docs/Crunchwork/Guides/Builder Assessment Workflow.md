Builder Assessment Workflow
A Builder Assessment job follows a structured workflow from job allocation through to assessment submission and invoicing.

 

The process includes the following stages:

Job allocation

Customer contact

Booking the site attendance

Attending the property

Submitting the assessment

Invoicing the Insurer

Insurer Review (if auto-approval doesn’t apply)

The diagram below illustrates the flow of a Builder Assessment job. Click the image to enter full screen mode. 

 


 

Workflow Stages
1. Job Allocation
The Builder Assessment job is created and allocated to your company by the Insurer.

 

At this stage:

The job appears in your job list, and an email notification is issued to you

Core claim data is automatically populated

The Request Date field is populated

A Purchase Order for the report fee is created

2. Customer Contact
The next step is to contact the customer to arrange an assessment appointment.

When you record that the customer has been contacted:

3. Booking the Site Attendance
Once you have agreed on a time with the customer, the site inspection can be booked.

4. Site Attendance
The next stage is attending the property to assess the damage.

 

During the attendance, you should:

Inspect the damage

Take photos if required

Prepare the information needed for your report or quote 

Automation Trigger

Action

System Behaviour

Attendance Date is set

Submission Due Date is automatically calculated

The Submission Due Date indicates the Insurer’s expected timeframe for submitting your report or quote.

 

5. Submitting the Assessment
Once your report or quote is ready, it must be submitted to the Insurer.

 

Before submission, ensure that:

Claim Recommendation is completed

All required documentation has been uploaded

Automation Trigger

Action

System Behaviour

Assessment report/quote is submitted for the first time

First Submission Date is automatically populated

Assessment report/quote is resubmitted

Last Submission Date is automatically updated

 
The First Submission Date records when the assessment was initially provided to the Insurer.

 

The Last Submission Date records the most recent submission.

 

6. Invoicing the Assessment
Once your assessment has been submitted to the Insurer, you will need to then submit your invoice.

 

Automation Trigger

Action

System Behaviour

Invoice amount is the same as/below the PO amount

Allows invoice submission

 
7. Insurer Review
Once the assessment has been submitted, and if auto-approval did not apply, the Insurer will review the report and determine the next steps for the claim.

 

Possible outcomes may include:

Approval of repairs

Request for further information

Claim decision

Important Notes About Automations
Some fields within the Builder Assessment job are automatically populated by system automations.

 

These fields include:

Contact Date

Booked Date

Attendance Due Date

Attendance Date

Submission Due Date

First Submission Date

Last Submission Date

⚠️ These fields should not be manually edited.

They are automatically updated when the corresponding actions are completed in the workflow.
