Builder Make Safe Workflow
A Builder Make Safe job follows a structured workflow from job allocation through to make safe completion and invoicing.

The process includes the following stages:

Job allocation

Customer contact

Booking the site attendance

Attending the property

Completing the make safe works

Submitting the make safe quote

Invoicing the Insurer

Workflow Stages
1. Job Allocation
The Builder Make Safe job is created and allocated to your company by the Insurer, or created by you if a make safe requirement is identified during another job.

At this stage:

The job appears in your job list, and an email notification is issued to you

Core claim data is automatically populated

The Request Date field is populated

Once the job has been received, the next step is to contact the customer to arrange attendance.

 

2. Customer Contact
The next step is to contact the customer to arrange a make safe attendance.

When you record that the customer has been contacted:

3. Booking the Site Attendance
Once you have agreed on a time with the customer, the make safe attendance can be booked.

This confirms the make safe attendance has been scheduled.

 

4. Site Attendance
At the scheduled time, you or your nominated trade should attend the property to complete the make safe works.

Typical make safe actions may include:

Temporary repairs to prevent further damage

Securing unsafe areas of the property

Temporary weatherproofing

Isolating unsafe building elements

Once the required works have been completed, the job can progress to quote submission.

 

5. Completing the Make Safe Works
After the make safe works have been completed at the property, the completion must be recorded in the job.

 

If the make safe is no longer required, setting Make Safe Required to No will cancel the Make Safe job.

 

6. Submitting the Make Safe Quote
Once the make safe works have been completed, you must submit your make safe quote to the Insurer.

 

If auto-approval does not apply, the Insurer will review the quote and determine whether it can be approved.

Important Notes About Automations
Some fields within the Builder Make Safe job are automatically populated by system automations. These fields include:

Contact Date

Booked Date

Attendance Due Date

Attendance Date

⚠️ These fields should not be manually edited.

They are automatically updated when the corresponding actions are completed in the workflow.
